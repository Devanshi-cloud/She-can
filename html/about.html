About Us

How it started?

She Can Foundation was founded by a group of individuals who shared a common vision of creating a world where every woman has the opportunity to thrive and succeed. The idea for the organization was born out of a desire to make a real difference in the lives of women in communities across the globe. We recognized that there are countless barriers that prevent women from reaching their full potential, and we wanted to create an organization that could help break down those barriers and provide women with the resources and support they need to succeed. With a shared passion and a determination to create positive change, we set out to make our vision a reality, and She Can Foundation was born.

What is She Can?

She Can Foundation is a non-profit organization dedicated to empowering women and creating a more equitable society. We provide support, resources, and training to women in communities across the globe, and we raise awareness of women's issues through advocacy campaigns and initiatives. We believe that every woman deserves the opportunity to thrive and succeed, regardless of her background or circumstances, and we work tirelessly to create a world where that is possible. We are committed to breaking down barriers and creating lasting change, and we invite you to join us in our mission to empower women and create a better future for all.

what we do

Our Core Focus & Impact Pillars

Education & Literacy

Providing foundational tutoring and academic support to young students, helping build strong scholastic foundations.

Digital Learning

Equipping children and youth with basic and advanced computer literacy, tech workshops, and digital safety habits.

Rights & Advocacy

Conducting interactive workshops on gender equality, women's legal rights, safety education, and social awareness.

Leadership & Livelihood

Fostering career guidance, entrepreneurial mentoring, self-reliance, and financial independence skills for women.

our history

Our Journey & Milestones

2021

The Spark

A passionate group of youth and changemakers united with a shared vision of social empowerment. We conducted our first local tutoring circles and safety mentorship drives under trees and in basic community corridors.

2022

Foundation & Registration

Officially registered the NGO under the Indian Society Act, 1860. Launched dedicated basic literacy curricula and formalized academic classes for adolescent girl groups across multiple local clusters.

2023

Digital & Infrastructure Expansion

Introduced computing basics, digital security training, and keyboard fluency by acquiring community computer tables and laptops, enabling underprivileged girls to gain hands-on tech familiarity.

2024 - Present

Scale, Certifications & Future Vision

Obtained critical tax-exempt statuses (12A & 80G registrations) to formalize institutional support. We continue expanding our footprint, training hundreds of students annually, and championing women's advocacy.

A youth-driven NGO registered under the Indian Society Act, 1860. Empowering women and transforming lives through education, digital initiatives, and community-driven programs.
